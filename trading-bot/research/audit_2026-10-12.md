## Area 1 — BTC T50 (long if close > SMA50, else flat)

**Reproduced exactly** (Binance BTCUSDT 2017-08-17 → 2026-10-07, 3339 days, no gaps/dups): Sharpe 1.29 vs 0.83 B&H,
maxDD −64% vs −83%, x81.9 vs x19.4, 19.6 flips/yr, 56% of days long. Independent manual recomputation (no repo helpers)
gives the same Sharpe/multiple/DD, so there is no look-ahead: position decided at close *t* (SMA includes close *t*) is
applied to the close-to-close return *t→t+1* (`research_daily_factors.py:80-84,100-104`; `research_multi_cycle.py:147-151`).
Fee 0.10%/side charged on every position change; SMA warm-up handled via NaN masking; B&H compared on the identical index.
Survivorship is not a concern for a single-asset rule (the alt universe does include delisted coins and is point-in-time; Study A
reproduced 0/8 passes too).

Findings (ranked):

1. **The multi-cycle "pre-registration" is not an independent confirmation (methodology, medium).** I ran the first-stage
   Evedex study (`research_daily_factors.py`, cache 2019-09 → 2026-10): T50 was already the *only* variant of 4 that
   "PRAEINA" there (Sharpe 1.19/1.48 vs B&H 0.61/1.15). Commits show that study was executed at 09:37 and the multi-cycle
   prereg written at 10:12 the same day (`4cb0cc9`/`d17ea31` → `28db608`). The multi-cycle sample 2017-08 → 2026-10 contains
   that 2019-09 → 2026 window (≈7 of 9 years); the only new data is 2017-08 → 2019-09. The prereg itself says the
   variants were chosen before any return was computed — true for the *first* study, not for the second.
   Impact: the pass is genuine but should be described as "BTC trend-following (a well-known effect) reproduced on
   overlapping data", not "survived an independent out-of-sample test".
2. **Pass criteria are lenient (methodology, medium).** Over the full sample 11 of 13 SMA lengths from 30 to 150 pass the
   registered B-criteria; "maxDD < B&H" is near-automatic for any long/flat filter on an asset with −83% DD, and
   "Sharpe > 0.8" is barely above B&H's own 0.83. Only "Sharpe > B&H in 2 of 3 regimes" has bite. Good news: the result is
   *not* a parameter fluke (T40–T70: Sharpe 1.07–1.30, all pass; fee 0.5%/side still Sharpe 1.12).
3. **Statistical strength is modest and regime-concentrated (interpretation, medium).** Sharpe difference 0.46; joint
   30-day block bootstrap 90% CI [0.07, 0.84], P(diff ≤ 0) = 2.5%. Alpha vs beta-matched BTC (β = 0.49): +33%/yr, t = 2.97.
   Outperformance comes from 2018 (−43% vs −73%), 2021, 2022 (−50% vs −64%); the strategy **lagged B&H in 2019, 2023, 2024**
   and since 2023-01 is x3.6 vs x3.4 with −26% vs −53% DD. From 2021-01: Sharpe 0.73 vs 0.40, x3.1 vs x1.5. B&H daily
   mean when the rule is LONG +0.28%, when FLAT −0.01%. In P2 (2021-22) the strategy's own Sharpe is 0.37 (+13% over two
   years) — it "won" only because B&H was −43%. Expectation to set: beta with a drawdown brake, ~20 round-trips/yr, lagging
   in bull years; the user's own docstring says this correctly.
4. **Paper tracker (`analysis/paper_tracker.py`) — correct (read + tests).** Flip detection, one SIDE_FEE per flip (= one
   side), equity compounding with the prior day's position, idempotent `last_day` processing, atomic state write are all
   right; the 5 tests exercise them. Verified live that Evedex 1d candle timestamps are candle-*open* UTC, so
   `s.index < today` (line 52) correctly drops the running candle. Evedex BTCUSD perp vs Binance spot closes differ by
   0.046% median; the T50 state disagrees on 1 of 2539 days — negligible. Fee 0.04% vs study 0.10% is documented.
   Minor: `CANDLES_LIMIT = 200` days is enough for SMA50 but the tracker cannot back-fill a gap longer than 200 days.

## Area 2 — BTC/ETH option premium (VRP) rules

**Reproduced:** BTC H1 VRP +8.44 vol pts, NW t 5.44 (pass); H2 naked straddle +0.87%/30d, t 0.89, 3/6 years (fail);
H3 LONG−FLAT +0.54%, t 0.43 (fail) → NEPRAEINA. ETH: high-IV straddle +2.90%, t 1.71; high−low +5.24%, t 2.26;
2/3 years → H4a/H4b fail → NEPRAEINA. Prereg criteria were applied as written. Black-Scholes proxy is correct
(`atm_call_pct` = 2N(σ√T/2) − 1 is the exact ATM r=0 call; matches 0.7979·σ·√T to 0.01%); RV window is t+1…t+30 (checked);
point-in-time percentile is correct and tested; IV/price alignment at daily close is consistent.

Findings (ranked):

1. **Bug: T50 flag in H3 uses a truncated SMA (`research_options_vrp.py:74-83`, used at line 150) — low, direction favours
   passing.** `sma50` is computed on the price series *after* the inner-join with DVOL (starts 2021-03-24), so the first 49
   days have SMA NaN → `(px > NaN)` = FLAT. 32 of those days were actually LONG (BTC Mar–May 2021, then the May crash
   lands in the "FLAT" bucket). `sma_ok` is computed but never used to filter. Corrected H3: diff +0.09%, t 0.08 (vs
   +0.54%, 0.43 as coded). Verdict unchanged (fails either way), but the bias was in favour of the filter.
2. **ETH verdict is threshold-fragile — "not proven", not "refuted" (interpretation, medium).** With the registered
   tercile cut-offs (≥0.667 / <0.333, 365-day window): t 1.71 / 2.26. With ≥0.75 / <0.25: t 2.57 / 2.66 — both would clear
   2.5 (the years criterion would still have to be checked). Window 252 d: 1.57 / 1.87; 500 d: 1.96 / 2.04. Non-overlapping
   high-IV t over 30 phase offsets ranges −0.7 … 3.2 (median 1.1). Mid-IV bucket is −3.7% (non-monotonic) → noise. The BTC
   point-in-time replication (descriptive) gives t 2.93 / 3.43 but only 2 qualifying years. The right reading is low power
   with a positive point estimate (+2.9%/30d, 70% of high-IV days positive, p5 −27%, worst −57%).
3. **The premium exists; the chosen implementation is what fails (decision-relevant).** H1 passes at t 5.4 (IV exceeds
   subsequent RV by 8.4 pts on average). A delta-hedged straddle proxy (vega × (IV−RV) − 3% cost) gives +1.5%/30d of spot,
   NW t 4.35, 5/6 years positive; a variance-swap-like proxy t 3.8 — **before hedging costs**, which in crypto are material.
   A naked 30-day straddle held to expiry bundles that premium with |30-day return| noise, which is why H2 fails. If the
   user wants to pursue options, the honest next step is a *new* prereg of a hedged/defined-risk structure, not re-reading
   this one.
4. **Modelling assumptions bias toward passing (low; conservative for the negative verdict).** DVOL is a model-free
   30-day index including wings, typically a few vol points above ATM IV → ATM straddle premium overstated. 3% of premium
   is a low all-in cost for Deribit BTC options. NW lag 30 is the minimum for 30-day overlapping windows, but t changes
   < 0.15 for lags 45–90 (straddle P&L ACF: 0.94 at lag 1, −0.28 at lag 30). None of these could flip "fail" to "pass".

## Area 3 — Reddit strategy table (S1,S3,S7,S8,S11,S12,S15)

**Reproduced:** main-window numbers identical to `results.txt` / `results_stocks.txt` from both 1h caches (the two caches
differ in 1 of 80 016 hourly closes). Indicators checked against TradingView conventions: EMA (span, adjust=False), RMA
(α=1/n), RSI, Wilder ATR, DMI/ADX(13), WMA, BB with population stdev — all correct for a full-history warm-up. Execution:
signal on closed bar → next open, intrabar SL checked before TP, gap → open, close-based exits → next open, 1% risk with
≤100% notional, 0.1%/side + 2-tick slippage, forced close at window end, no overlapping positions, DD on the close-marked
equity curve. `verdict()` and the hold-out "РАЗВАЛИЛАСЬ" rule match the prereg exactly. No parameter was tuned, so there is
no hold-out leakage.

Findings (ranked):

1. **Hold-out verdicts are noise (interpretation, medium).** S3/BTC is "РАЗВАЛИЛАСЬ" (3 trades, PF 0.77) on the Oct-11
   cache and "держится" (2 trades, PF 1.48) on the Oct-8 cache — one trade over 2026-10-08/09 flips the label. S3/SPY's
   hold-out is 13 trades. The informative statistic is the bootstrap PF 5th percentile on the main window: < 1 for every
   "РАБОТАЕТ" strategy (S3/SPY 0.84). Also S3/SPY: +5.9% in 2.5 years at 1% risk vs +57% B&H. The conclusion "nothing
   usable" is correct and the README states it.
2. **Minor look-ahead in two entry filters, Python ≠ Pine (low, no verdict impact).** `research_reddit_strats.py:245` (S12)
   requires `d.o[i+1] > e200[i+1]`, where `e200[i+1]` includes `close[i+1]`, unknown at that open; lines 339-342 (S1) use
   `entry = d.o[i+1]` to decide whether to take the signal (TP above / stop below the *next* open). Neither filter exists in
   the Pine files. S12 is МАЛО ДАННЫХ (20 trades) and S1 has PF 0.49, so the verdicts cannot move. S11 Python checks the SL
   on the fill bar (line 216) while Pine's `strategy.exit` only activates from the next bar → Python slightly more pessimistic.
   The Pine scripts were never executed (stated in the prereg); treat them as untested.
3. **Daily bars built from 1h drop incomplete UTC days (low).** Inside the test window only 2023-03-24 (23 hours) is dropped;
   negligible, but the "1d" series is not identical to Binance's official daily candle on that day.
4. Costs for stocks (0.1%/side + $0.02) are conservative relative to real US equity costs — direction: against passing;
   does not matter given the results.

## Area 4 — Telegram group signals (kralov)

**Reproduced from the raw HTML exports:** 3435 signals, 8281 updates parsed; M0 n = 3298, gross −0.005R, net −0.064R/signal,
day-clustered t −1.80, all three thirds negative; anti-signal also negative (−0.043R) → NEPRAEINA. Timezone offset
independently verified: zone overlaps the candle at T for 99.2% of signals at −3h vs 54–67% at −2h/−4h. Candle cache: 42
instruments, 2026-04-26 → 2026-10-09, no gaps.

Findings (ranked):

1. **Criterion 3 ("direction skill", TP3 z = 16.8) is censoring-biased and should not be cited (medium).**
   `resolve_levels()` (`research_kralov_eval.py:83-96`) classifies a tranche only if TP *or* SL was touched before the
   forced exit (opposite signal / 7 days); otherwise `None` and it is excluded from the hit rate. Only 45% of signals are
   resolved for TP3 (64% for TP1, 25% for TP5). The censored trades are losers: mean gross −0.29R (TP3), −0.41R (TP1).
   Because the group issues the opposite signal precisely when a trade is going against it, censoring removes adverse paths
   and inflates hit rates. The ladder EV implied by the resolved-only hit rates is **+0.40R**, the actual gross is **−0.005R**.
   The z-statistic also assumes independence across ~22 highly correlated signals/day, so it is inflated further. The P&L
   criteria (1, 2) include censored outcomes correctly, so the overall "fail" is right; the prereg's criterion 3 was simply a
   bad test, and it "passing" is not evidence of skill.
2. **Limit-fill model (mode L) is optimistic on fills but the result is insensitive (low).** Fill assumed whenever the bar
   low touches the zone middle (no queue/trade-through), at `min(mid, open)`; TPs are not checked on the fill bar
   (conservative); 308 "missed" (ran to TP1 before fill) and 87 unfilled are dropped — the classic limit-order
   selection effect, correctly reported. Net −0.060R ≈ M0, so the conclusion does not depend on the fill model. Prereg says
   "unfilled if SL hit first"; the code instead fills and takes −1R (pessimistic vs prereg) — descriptive only.
3. **Skip categories (low).** `invalid_sl` (16, price already through SL at entry → certain losers removed, favours the
   group), `stale` (68), `open` (35, last 7 days — time-based, unbiased). All counted and printed; 0.5% of signals.
4. Costs: 0.06%/side + 10%/yr funding ≈ 0.06R per signal (median risk 3.8%). Lower costs would favour passing; irrelevant.

## What would change the user's decisions

- **T50:** keep the paper tracker, but do not describe it as independently validated or as alpha. Realistic expectation:
  roughly B&H in bull years (lagging in 2023–24), large relative gains only in bear years, ~20 round-trips/yr. The
  Sharpe edge over B&H is real but marginal (P ≈ 2.5%); drawdowns of −58% to −64% remain.
- **Options:** the data say the variance risk premium is there (t 5.4) and that *naked* straddle/put selling is a poor
  way to collect it. "Crypto options direction closed" is too strong; a hedged/defined-risk structure with realistic
  hedging costs is the untested question (new prereg, not a re-analysis of this one). The ETH high-IV result is
  borderline, not negative.
- **Reddit / kralov:** nothing to act on; conclusions stand. Stop quoting TP-hit percentages from the group (or from
  the evaluator) — they are censored statistics.
